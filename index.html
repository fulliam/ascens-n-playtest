Language

Gameplay
Movement Input
Auto uses a floating touch joystick on phones/tablets and keyboard (WASD / arrows) elsewhere.
Vibration
Short haptic pulse on hits, level-ups and death.
Auto-Pause
Pause the run automatically when you switch away from this tab or window.
Local co-op
Co-op Rewards
Shared splits every XP, gold and crystal orb evenly across the party, so each player levels and earns at the solo pace. Separate gives the whole orb to whoever picked it up.
Second Keyboard Player
Splits the keyboard: WASD stays with player 1, the arrow keys plus Enter and right Shift become player 2. Also on F2. Extra gamepads join with L1+R1 during a run.
Key bindings
Bound keys glow on the board. Arrow keys stay with the second keyboard player.
Display
Camera Angle
How the world is tilted. Top-down is flat and reads fastest; isometric and oblique tilt the plane. Movement follows the screen at any angle.
Fullscreen
Expand the game to fill your entire screen.
Performance
Graphics Quality
Lower settings reduce glow and particle effects.
Enemy Density
Caps how many enemies can exist at once. Independent of graphics quality — lower this only if the game is slowing down, not for a harder/easier fight.
Debug Overlay
Shows FPS, frame time, and live entity counts — useful for diagnosing slowdowns.
Effects
Screen Shake
Camera shake on hits and impacts.
Damage Numbers
Show floating damage numbers on hits.
Reduce Flashing
Softer, less synchronized hit-flash when many enemies are struck at once.
Map
Minimap
Map of the explored area with chests, towers and portals. Click it to change its size.
Chest Pointers
Arrows at the screen edge toward found chests, with the distance.
